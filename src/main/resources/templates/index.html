How it works

Demand first, then the ride.

Students say where they're going

Pick a pickup point and destination. No booking, no waiting for a single auto.

Requests cluster into a hotspot

Everyone on the same route forms one live demand cluster drivers can see.

Drivers reserve seats

Driver A takes 5, Driver B takes 5 — the hotspot closes by itself.

Ride, complete, remember

Contacts are revealed only after acceptance; every trip is saved to history.

Demand clustering

Row-locked seat allocation means two drivers can never claim the same student.

Anonymous until matched

Drivers see route totals only. Names and numbers appear after a ride is confirmed.

Companion rides

Any student with a vehicle can offer spare seats to classmates on the same route.

Verified drivers

OTP-verified accounts, and the transport office approves every vehicle.
